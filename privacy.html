Privacy policy

Ad Astra Dashboard. Last updated 18 October 2026.

Who uses this app

Ad Astra Dashboard is a private tool used only by its owner. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What it accesses in Google

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Google Drive. It reads and saves files in the owner's business folder there: the dashboard's data file, the owner's income and expenses workbook, backups, and documents the owner creates in the app (such as invoices and quotations). It does not access anyone else's Google account or data.

Where the data goes

The data stays in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own computers. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

The one exception is the app's optional AI assistant. When the owner asks it a question, a summary of the relevant business records is sent to the AI service the owner has chosen (currently Google Gemini) to answer that question.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access to Google Drive at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
